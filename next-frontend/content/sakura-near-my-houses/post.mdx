I moved to three different houses during my time in Japan, and each neighborhood had its own unique sakura scenery. This is a look back at those memories.

## I. Yōga (用賀) — March 25, 2023

These images are from Kinuta Park (砧公園), a huge, vibrant park with many sakura trees. I lived with a friend in a LeoPalace very close to the park. This was my first time seeing sakura at such a huge scale.

## II. Naruse (成瀬) — April 12, 2024

Naruse is famous for the many sakura trees planted along both banks of the Onda River (恩田川). I went for a morning walk before heading to the office. Most of the flowers had already started falling and fresh leaves were appearing, but the scene was still beautiful.
